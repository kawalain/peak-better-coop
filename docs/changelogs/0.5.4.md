# 0.5.4

2026-11-09

The first release on [Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/peak/p/kawalain/BetterCoop/).
Everyone in the room needs 0.5.4.

Climb PEAK in Pairs: one player carries the other for the whole Run, and the
two steer that one body together, the **Carrier** on the keyboard and the
**Rider** on the mouse. They share stamina and statuses, and they live, die,
and come back together.

- Both players see through the Carrier's eyes, with the same HUD, crosshair,
  item sounds, and backpack wheel; the Rider picks from the wheel.
- Pairs change when a campfire is lit, and optionally on a timer. A Pair can
  trade roles with **Swap Places** in the ESC menu. Items and statuses carry
  over to the new Carrier.
- The Troop Leader (host) sets the Run Rules for the room: odd player counts,
  Campfire and Timed Shuffles, Body Handover of place and items, carry weight,
  and who picks from the backpack wheel.
- The Troop Leader presses F8 to re-attach every Pair.
- The party departs only with an even number of players, unless the Troop
  Leader allows an odd one, and only when everyone has the same version.
- BetterCoop turns itself off beside Co-op Mod, and after a game update it
  does not fit yet.
