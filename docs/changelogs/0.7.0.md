# 0.7.0

2026-11-10

Rescue Carry lets a Pair carry a passed-out scout to safety, the paper
forms and notices look and move like the real thing, and the mod works with
the latest PEAK update. Players need 0.7.0 to be paired with a 0.7.0 Troop
Leader.

## Breaking changes

- 0.6.x players are not paired in a 0.7.0 room: they depart and climb as
  Unmodded Players.

## New

- **Rescue Carry** (a Run Rule, on by default): a Pair, or a player without
  one, picks up a passed-out scout as in the plain game; the Pair's Rider
  carries them. The back item drops where they lay, and the back-slot key or
  picking up a backpack puts them down. A Pair carries one scout at a time.
- The Run Report counts rescue time, and the **Rescue Ranger** badge goes to
  whoever rescued the longest.

## Changed

- The Rescue Ranger takes the Climbing King's place: nearly everyone earned
  it. The Steady Back badge is now a rucksack.
- Height climbed no longer counts while the Shared Body is passed out.
- Under the Control Split, grabbing and letting go of a wall follows the
  Rider's own Climb Input setting (Hold or Toggle).
- Forms are printed after the 1959 Boy Scout Handbook: trail-sign headings,
  typed values, tables ruled as an advancement chart, numbered serials, and
  photos developed as in a darkroom and mounted in album corners.
- Stamps leave real inked impressions and dry away in soft patches;
  telegrams come off a teleprinter and memos off a ditto drum.

## Fixed

- After the latest PEAK update, a Body Handover moved no one: the game now
  takes warps only from the Troop Leader, who now sends them.
- A Rider now dies at once with their Carrier however the Carrier dies, and
  no longer dies from a kill the game refused.
