# 0.7.0

2026-11-10

Rescue Carry lets a Pair carry a passed-out scout to safety, the paper
forms and notices look and move like the real thing, the mod holds up under
large parties and players who misbehave, and it works with the latest PEAK
update. Players need 0.7.0 to be paired with a 0.7.0 Troop Leader.

## Breaking changes

- 0.6.x players are not paired in a 0.7.0 room: they depart and climb as
  Unmodded Players.

## New

- **Rescue Carry** (a Run Rule, on by default): a Pair, or a player without
  one, picks up a passed-out scout as in the plain game; the Pair's Rider
  carries them. The back item drops where they lay, and the back-slot key or
  picking up a backpack puts them down. A Pair carries one scout at a time.
- The Run Report counts rescue time, and the **Rescue Ranger** badge goes to
  whoever rescued the longest.

## Changed

- The Rescue Ranger takes the Climbing King's place: nearly everyone earned
  it. The Steady Back badge is now a rucksack.
- Height climbed no longer counts while the Shared Body is passed out.
- Under the Control Split, grabbing and letting go of a wall follows the
  Rider's own Climb Input setting (Hold or Toggle).
- Late Pairing pairs every two players without a partner at once, so a party
  joining together is paired in one countdown, and each player's letter
  names their partner. Two who are down, or an odd one out, wait for the
  next.
- With more than 64 players who can pair in the room, the first 64 to join
  are paired and the rest climb without a Pair until a place frees up;
  everyone is told once. Before, so large a party could not be paired at all.
- The Troop Leader's Roster orders go through at most once a second, and a
  player's Role Swap ask likewise; the Roster says when an order came too
  soon, and that a shuffle waiting behind a Role Swap or Late Pairing will
  run.
- A Role Swap, Late Pairing, Timed Shuffle or Campfire Shuffle counting down
  is called off when the Troop Leader turns off the rule that started it.
- Forms are printed after the 1959 Boy Scout Handbook: headings over printed
  rules, typed values, tables ruled as an advancement chart, numbered
  serials, and photos developed as in a darkroom and mounted in album
  corners.
- Stamps leave real inked impressions and dry away in soft patches;
  telegrams come off a teleprinter and memos off a ditto drum.

## Fixed

- After the latest PEAK update, a Body Handover moved no one: the game now
  takes warps only from the Troop Leader, who now sends them.
- A Rider now dies at once with their Carrier however the Carrier dies, and
  no longer dies from a kill the game refused, nor from a death of their
  Carrier sent by another player.
- A Pair Shuffle, Role Swap, Late Pairing or Pair Assignment was undone when
  a member left just after it, though places, items and statuses had already
  moved.
- A Troop Leader who lost the lead and took it back without leaving could
  pair a later Run from the Pairs of an earlier one.
- A long Run with many players coming and going, or with frequent shuffles,
  could make every player lose the Pairs, or the Carry Record and Run Report.
- A Rider clicking or scrolling faster than the Carrier's frames (a high
  frame rate, a macro, a free-spinning wheel) kept the Shared Body using
  items or switching slots long after they stopped.
- The Rider's buttons, the crosshair, the backpack wheel and item sounds are
  sent no more often than needed, so many Pairs no longer press on the
  room's message limits.
- A Role Swap asked and withdrawn again and again no longer fills the
  partner's screen with notices or holds back the others.
- A player joining mid-Run on a slow computer was announced to everyone as
  having no mod while the island loaded.
- A Pair whose carried scout left the room counted as rescuing until the Run
  ended, and could win the Rescue Ranger.
