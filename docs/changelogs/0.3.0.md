# 0.3.0

2026-11-09

Pairs change during the Run. Everyone in the room needs 0.3.0.

## New

- **Pair Shuffle**: new Pairs form when a campfire is lit (Campfire Shuffle)
  and, optionally, on a timer (Timed Shuffle), after a three-second countdown.
- **Body Handover**: each new Shared Body takes over the place and items of
  the former body it shares the most with.
- **Role Swap**: a Pair trades roles when both press Swap Places in the pause
  menu.
- A Carrier can wear and hold a back item; the Rider's hands and back stay
  empty.
- The Rider sees the Shared Body's status effects, gets prompts and progress
  for their own actions, and looks through the binoculars.

## Fixed

- The Rider lives exactly while the Carrier does, even when a revival arrives
  before the death.
- The Rider's HUD shows the Shared Body after a revival, before the Carrier
  picks them up again.
