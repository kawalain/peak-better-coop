# PEAK Co-op

A PEAK mod in which players climb in two-person teams: one player carries the
other for the whole climb, and the two share stamina, death, and revival.

## Language

### Runs

**Run**:
One expedition, from departing at the Airport check-in kiosk until the party
is back at the Airport.
_Avoid_: game, match, session, level

### Teams

**Pair**:
Two players bound together for a Run, one as Carrier and one as Rider.
_Avoid_: partner, couple, team

**Carrier**:
The member of a Pair who carries the other and controls the body's movement.
_Avoid_: host, driver

**Rider**:
The member of a Pair who is carried and controls the Carrier's hands.
_Avoid_: climber, passenger, carried player

**Role Preference**:
A player's stated wish to be Carrier, Rider, or either (Random) when Pairs are
formed.

**Unpaired Player**:
A player in the room who is not in any Pair and plays as in unmodded PEAK.
_Avoid_: solo player, odd player
